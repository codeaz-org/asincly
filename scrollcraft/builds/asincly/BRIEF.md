# BRIEF — Asincly landing (Living ribbon)

Interviewed. Verbatim answers below where provided.

## 1. Vibe (3–5 words + refs)

> Quiet, timezoned, humane, unhurried.
>
> - Lost in Translation (Sofia Coppola) — jet-lagged intimacy across time zones.
> - Kinfolk magazine — generous whitespace, human warmth without startup gloss.
> - A Muji notebook — soft, functional, almost invisible design.

## 2. Scroll journey (their words)

> - Open on a slow world-clock: horizontal strip of names/cities, half dim (asleep), a few
>   lit (awake, in-window), one glowing (checking in now). No headline yet — just the team
>   breathing.
> - Headline resolves out of the strip: "Standups that respect sleep."
> - One check-in materializes: phone-shaped card, member records a 40-sec clip, note
>   appears, then collapses into a tidy bullet summary — the whole "video → text" magic in
>   one gesture.
> - The occurrence view: today's digest, five members, five summaries, action items pulled
>   out, one blocker highlighted. Reader realizes: this replaces the meeting.
> - Timezone strip returns, now animated across a full day — sun sweeping, members lighting
>   up in their own local morning, digest assembling itself by evening.
> - Trust rung: self-host, AGPL, encrypted, RLS — stated plainly, not badge-spammed.
> - Close on the strip again, one CTA: "Start your team's morning."

## 3. Energy curve

> Low → low → gentle lift (the transform moment) → medium (digest reveal) → low+wide (day
> sweep, the emotional peak is quiet, not loud) → flat trust → whisper CTA. No crescendo.
> The intensity is in clarity, not volume.

## 4. Feeling curve + peak

> Stages: curiosity → recognition ("oh, that's my team") → relief (the transform) → calm
> ("I don't have to be there") → belonging (day sweep) → trust → resolve.

**The peak:**

> The day-sweep. Watching the sun cross the strip while teammates light up in their own
> mornings and the digest quietly writes itself. That's the feeling to sell — your team
> works without you having to be awake for it.

## 5. One thing no other site does

> A live team clock strip as the primary hero element — not decoration, the actual product
> surface. It's a real 24-hour ribbon of humans; scrolling scrubs time; hovering a person
> shows their local time and window state; it is the demo, the nav, and the emotional
> anchor. Every section returns to it in a different state. The site is the strip;
> everything else orbits it.

## 6. Range

> Premium-minimal, nudged one notch toward editorial. Kinfolk over Linear. Serif display
> for one or two moments (headline, peak), sans everywhere else. Not brutalist, not playful.

## 7. Worldflight or scenes

> Worldflight — one continuous 24-hour day. The strip is the ground; scrolling is time
> passing; sections are hours of that day, not chapters. You never leave the world.

## 8. Assets

> Nothing. Fully CSS/SVG-constructed: the strip, avatars as monograms, the phone card, the
> sun arc, the digest — all typography, geometry, and motion. No stock photography, no
> product screenshots faked into laptops. The restraint is the aesthetic.

## Feeling curve (before the score)

Hours are the unit; each hour maps to a range of scroll progress, and the emotion is what
the reader should be feeling as the sun crosses that hour on the ribbon.

| Hours | Emotion | What causes it on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 04–06 | Curiosity | Ribbon appears in near-darkness; one dot barely glowing; no headline yet. |
| 06–08 | Recognition | Sun touches left edge; Sydney lights, then Tokyo. Headline resolves out of the ribbon: "Standups that respect sleep." |
| 09–11 | Relief (the transform) | A single teammate card materializes above the ribbon: recording icon → note → collapses into bullet summary. Video→text magic in one gesture. |
| 13–15 | Calm | The occurrence view: five summaries stack, action items pulled out, one blocker highlighted. The reader lands the thought: this replaces the meeting. |
| 17–19 | **Belonging — PEAK** | The full day sweep. Sun crosses the ribbon; teammates light in their own local mornings; digest self-assembles as evening arrives. Widest scroll span. Held. |
| 20–22 | Trust | Dusk. Ribbon settles. Small type: self-host, AGPL, encrypted, RLS. Stated plainly. |
| 23–24 | Resolve | Night. CTA embedded in the ribbon at the far right: "Start your team's morning." A quiet input, not a bright button. |

## Peak, as the sentence a visitor would say to a friend

> "There's this landing page where the sun literally crosses the screen and you watch your
> teammates check in one by one in their own mornings while the digest writes itself.
> Nobody is waiting on anybody. It's a whole day in thirty seconds."

The peak lives in the 17–19 range. It gets the largest scroll span on the page and the act
before it (the digest reveal) is quieter than it is.

## Tell-someone sentence

> It's the site where scrolling *is* the day.

## Authored silences

- 04–06 opens with silence: no headline for the first screen. The ribbon breathing is the
  only content. Silence, not dead scroll.
- The peak (17–19) has no explanatory copy in its middle third. The composition speaks.
- The close (23–24) uses one line of type and one input. Everything else is the ribbon at
  rest.
